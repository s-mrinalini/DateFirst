## What we collect
- **Account data:** email, hashed password, age (derived from date of birth),
  city, gender, dating preferences.
- **Profile data:** first name, photo, bio, first-date idea you publish.
- **Communication data:** chat messages with matches, reports you file or
  receive, trusted-contact email addresses you provide.
- **Technical data:** hashed IP address, user-agent, session identifiers.
  We do not log full IPs.

## How we use it
- To match you with other users and to show your profile after a mutual like.
- To run safety features (verification, blocking, reporting, check-ins).
- To detect abuse, fraud, and CSAM in uploaded images.

## How we share it
- With other DateFirst users only as your privacy settings allow.
  Pre-match: name + photo + first-date idea. Post-match: full profile.
- With service providers strictly to operate the service: SendGrid (email),
  Twilio (SMS), AWS S3 (photo storage), Hive AI / Thorn / PhotoDNA
  (image safety scanning), MongoDB Atlas (database).
- With law enforcement when required by law (e.g., NCMEC reports for CSAM).

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)
- **Access / Export:** Settings → Export my data. We return a JSON archive
  of everything tied to your account.
- **Deletion:** Settings → Delete account. Data is purged after a 30-day
  grace period during which you can cancel.
- **Correction:** edit your profile any time.

## Retention
- Active accounts: data retained while the account exists.
- Deleted accounts: hard-purged 30 days after deletion request.
- Reports & moderation logs: retained 12 months for safety review.
